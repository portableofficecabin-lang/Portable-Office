## Why the 99 pages are a risk

- **Text shared with a sibling page:** 84.4–92.3% of each page's 5-word sequences also appear on its closest sibling. This was recomputed from the page text on `3827f98`, after #343. Each page is 516–564 words of body text.
- **No local proof:** none of the 99 has a local photo, project, address or distance. Only the product and place names change, which is the pattern Google's spam policies call doorway and scaled content.
- **Pairs that cannibalise each other:**
  - Porta Cabin and Portable Cabin, and Cargo Storage Container and Shipping Container, are the same product in the same catalogue category for the same place.
  - Container Office in Chennai repeats a city page's own intent (`/cities-we-serve/container-office-in-chennai`). Labour Colony in Bangalore does not: your keyword map of 5 Sep 2026 gives it "labour colony in Bangalore" and gives the Labour Camp city page "labour camp setup".
  - The owner's rule is one page per product × place.
- **Where the business delivers** (from `src/data/shippingZones.ts`):
  - Zone 1 is free (PIN codes starting 560, 562, 563 and 635).
  - Zone 2, the rest of Karnataka and Tamil Nadu: ₹18,000, 2–4 days.
  - Zone 3, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Kerala and Puducherry: ₹28,000, 3–5 days.
  - Zone 4, the rest of India: ₹45,000, 5–10 days.
  - All 177 city pages are in Karnataka (128), Tamil Nadu (48) and Andhra Pradesh (1).

Read-only query (Supabase → SQL editor). It returns counts only, with no personal data:

```sql
-- Page views and Call/WhatsApp clicks per promotion page, last 90 days
with v as (
  select page_path, count(*) as views, count(distinct visitor_id) as visitors,
         count(*) filter (where referrer ilike '%google.%') as views_from_google
  from public.page_views
  where page_path like '/promotions/%' and created_at >= now() - interval '90 days'
  group by page_path
), c as (
  select page_path, count(*) as call_or_whatsapp_clicks
  from public.click_events
  where page_path like '/promotions/%' and created_at >= now() - interval '90 days'
    and (element_text ilike '%call%' or element_text ilike '%whatsapp%' or element_text like '%97318%')
  group by page_path
)
select coalesce(v.page_path, c.page_path) as page_path, v.views, v.visitors, v.views_from_google, c.call_or_whatsapp_clicks
from v full join c on c.page_path = v.page_path
order by v.views desc nulls last;
```

## How each page was classified

The same rules were applied to all 99 pages, in this order:

0. **Your own keyword maps win.** `labour-colony-in-bangalore` is kept and marked Improve. On 5 Sep 2026 you assigned it "labour colony in Bangalore"; that map is recorded in `src/data/cityPages.ts`.
1. **Consolidate into a city page** when a city page already owns the same product × place: `container-office-in-chennai` → `/cities-we-serve/container-office-in-chennai`. The Site Office Container in Chennai page goes there too, because it is the same office family.
2. **Consolidate a synonym into its sibling** in the core places:
   - Porta Cabin → Portable Cabin.
   - Cargo Storage Container → Shipping Container. In Bangalore the target is the hand-written shipping-container page.
   - The sibling is the stronger hub (more city-page links). The kept page should name the synonym as an alternative wording.
3. **Noindex every Bunker Bed container cabin page.** It is a niche product with no city pages and no inbound links, and nobody searches "bunker bed container cabin in <state>".
4. **Noindex West Bengal and Maharashtra** (zone 4): no city pages, projects, photos or local facts.
5. **Zone 3** (Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Hyderabad, Kerala): noindex, except a page that already has a real local child page. Only `shipping-container-in-andhra-pradesh`, parent of the Hydel Visakhapatnam page, qualifies; it is marked Improve.
6. **Keep** a core-place page that at least 5 city pages link to. It is working as a hub, and hiding it would strand those links.
7. **Improve** a core-place page with at least one same-product city page in its place but fewer than 5 links. A page with a single child is marked lower priority.
8. **Noindex** a core-place page with no same-product city page and no city links.

Notes on the columns:
- **Closest city page** is a city page of the same product family. It is an exact product × place match where one exists; otherwise the nearest one in that place by map distance, or, if none exists in that place, the nearest anywhere with its distance.
- **Closest category** is the catalogue category the page already links to.
